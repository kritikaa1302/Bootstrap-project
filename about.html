About Us

Welcome to IIMM, Ayodhya

"Empowering professionals through quality education, innovation and excellence in Materials Management."


The Indian Institute of Materials Management (IIMM), Ayodhya is a professional branch of the Indian Institute of Materials Management, dedicated to promoting excellence in Materials Management, Supply Chain Management, Procurement, Inventory Control, Logistics, and Operations Management. The institute provides a platform for students, academicians, researchers, and industry professionals to enhance their knowledge and skills through seminars, workshops, conferences, training programs, and professional certification courses. IIMM Ayodhya is committed to supporting the vision of "Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat" by developing competent professionals who contribute to the growth of industries and the nation's economy. With experienced faculty, industry experts, and modern learning opportunities, the institute continuously works towards bridging the gap between academic knowledge and practical industry requirements. The branch also encourages research, innovation, networking, and professional development while fostering ethical values, leadership, and lifelong learning among its members.
